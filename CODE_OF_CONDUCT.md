# Code of Conduct

## Our Community Values

RideWise aims to build a respectful, and collaborative open source community. Everyone should feel comfortable asking questions, sharing ideas, reporting problems, and contributing regardless of their background.

We value:

- Respect and kindness
- Open communication
- Collaboration
- Different ideas and perspectives
- Constructive feedback

## Expected Behavior

Community members are expected to:

- Treat others with respect.
- Communicate clearly and professionally.
- Give constructive feedback.
- Welcome and support new contributors.
- Respect different opinions and experience levels.
- Accept feedback and disagreements respectfully.
- Give credit to others for their contributions.

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following behavior is not acceptable:

- Discrimination or offensive comments.
- Insults, or intentionally disrespectful language.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all RideWise community spaces like GitHub Issues, Pull requests, Discussions, and many more.

It applies to anyone participating in the RideWise community.

## Reporting Violations

If you experience or observe behavior that violates this Code of Conduct, please report it privately to the project maintainer.

**Maintainer:** Kalyana Pabbisetty

Reports will be handled as privately as reasonably possible.

## Enforcement

Depending on the situation, actions may include:

1. A warning about unacceptable behavior.
2. Removal of inappropriate comments or content.
3. Permanent removal from the community for serious or repeated violations.

Enforcement decisions should be focused on maintaining a safe and productive community.

By participating in RideWise, you agree to follow this Code of Conduct and help create a respectful and welcoming community.
